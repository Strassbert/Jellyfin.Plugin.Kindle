Books you send are delivered to this address. Only you can see and change it.

Where do I find this address?

  1. Open your Amazon account and go to "Manage Your Content and Devices".
  2. Switch to "Preferences" and open "Personal Document Settings".
  3. Copy the address ending in @kindle.com and paste it above.
  4. In the same place, add your server's sender address to the approved list - Amazon silently drops mail from unapproved senders.
